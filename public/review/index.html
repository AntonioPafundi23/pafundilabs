How delivery works

A fixed process. A written outcome.

Suggested delivery is within 10 business days of kickoff and receipt of the required materials. The introductory call confirms fit and scope; it is not the paid engagement.

  1. 01

    Kickoff

    A 45-minute call to confirm objectives, constraints, materials and system access.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Independent review of the agreed product, systems, decisions and commercial context.

  3. 03

    Report

    Written findings, tangible recommendations and a practical sequence of next steps.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough

    A 60-minute session to explain the conclusions and answer questions.

What this is not

Clear boundaries keep the offer useful.

These assessments help you make a better-informed product or technology decision. They are independent: there is no incentive to sell a subsequent software project.

If you need implementation after the assessment, that is scoped and quoted separately.

Not included

  • Full security audits, penetration tests or compliance certifications
  • Exhaustive line-by-line code audits
  • Hands-on implementation, development or ongoing retainer work
  • Claims of product-market fit without customer evidence
  • Regulatory, legal or investment advice
