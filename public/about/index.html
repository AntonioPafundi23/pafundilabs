Antonio Pafundi / Founder

An engineer first.
An adviser who understands delivery.

Pafundi Labs helps businesses make clearer technology decisions — and put those decisions into practice.

Antonio’s background includes VP Engineering in Barclays Rates and trading technology experience at Man Group. He has led engineering modernisation across London, Paris and New York, working with real-time platforms, data flows and the operational demands of financial firms.

That experience informs a practical approach: understand the system and the people around it, make the important trade-offs clear, and focus effort where it will make a useful difference.

Pafundi Labs brings that approach to advisory work, engineering and automation. Clients work directly with Antonio, from the initial discussion through the agreed engagement.

What shapes the work

Judgement before technology.

01

Understand before prescribing

Existing systems usually have a history and a reason. Start by understanding both before recommending a replacement.

02

Make the trade-offs visible

Cost, delivery time, operational risk and maintainability all matter. Good advice makes those choices easier to assess.

03

Leave something useful

A clear decision, an actionable roadmap or a working improvement. The output should help the team move forward.

A useful first conversation

What are you trying to solve?

A technology decision, a system that needs attention, or an idea worth testing. Start with a few lines about the situation.
