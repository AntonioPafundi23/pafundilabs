The questions worth asking

Better priorities.
Before more complexity.

Start with a specific decision or operating problem. Establish what needs to change, what can stay, and how to make progress within the constraints.

What should the team automate first?

Review the manual hand-offs, reporting and reconciliation work to identify useful improvements.

Buy, build or improve what is there?

Assess vendor options and internal systems against the actual requirements and ability to maintain them.

What is making the platform harder to operate?

Examine architecture, data flow, failure modes and the engineering practices around the system.

Where would AI be useful?

Identify bounded applications with a clear purpose, appropriate data handling and human oversight.
